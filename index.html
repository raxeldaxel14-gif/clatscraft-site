What this is

Built by students, kept casual on purpose.

01

Survival, together

Classic SMP rules — build a base, team up or go solo, trade with other players. No pay-to-win, no ranks to buy.

02

Local, mostly

Made for Clatskanie students and friends first. If you know someone on the server, you're already halfway in.

03

Growing over time

New plugins, events, and features get added as we go. Follow the Discord to catch updates before they land.

Before you join

The short version of the rules.

  • Respect Be decent to other players. Harassment, hate speech, and targeted bullying get you removed.
  • Build Don't grief or steal from other players' builds or chests.
  • Play fair No cheat clients, X-ray, or exploiting bugs for an advantage.
  • Keep it clean No NSFW builds, symbols, or usernames.

Full rules and consequences are posted in Discord and in-game at /rules.
